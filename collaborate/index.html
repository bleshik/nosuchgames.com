Our principles

Clear terms. Creative freedom.

  • No required praise. Trying a build never creates an obligation to publish or be positive.
  • No hidden arrangements. Paid and affiliate relationships should be disclosed clearly.
  • No vague economics. Fees, tracking, rev share, usage rights, and timing are agreed in writing.
  • No empty spectacle. The collaboration should give the audience something genuinely interesting.
